# Autonomous Survey Boat

An autonomous surface vehicle (ASV) that maps shallow coastal water,
harbors, and lakes — bathymetry (sea floor depth) and water quality
(temperature, pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen) — for places that have
never been surveyed because sending a crewed boat isn't economical.

## Why

Greece has a huge amount of coastline and thousands of small harbors,
coves, and lakes with no modern depth map or water-quality baseline.
This data matters for navigation safety, environmental monitoring
(pollution, algae blooms), fisheries, and coastal engineering.

## Scope (this 6-month phase)

Surface vehicle only — no full submersion. A tethered/winched sensor
pod for closer inspection is an optional stretch goal. A fully
autonomous submersible (true AUV) is explicitly future work.
See `docs/PLAN.md` "Explicitly out of scope" for the full list of
what this phase does NOT attempt, and why.
